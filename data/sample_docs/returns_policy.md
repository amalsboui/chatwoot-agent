# Returns & Refunds Policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with
tags attached.

To start a return, go to Order History, select the order, and click
"Start a Return". You'll receive a prepaid shipping label by email within
a few minutes.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Shipping fees are
non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defect,
or damage in transit).

Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.

Exchanges: we currently do not support direct exchanges. Please return the
original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.

Damaged or defective items: contact support with photos of the damage
within 7 days of delivery for an expedited replacement or refund, no
return shipping required.
