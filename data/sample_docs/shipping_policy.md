# Shipping Policy

We offer three shipping tiers: Standard (5-7 business days, free over $50),
Express (2-3 business days, $9.99), and Overnight (1 business day, $24.99).

Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM
EST ship the next business day. We do not ship on weekends or public
holidays.

International shipping is available to Canada, the UK, and the EU. Customs
fees and import duties are the responsibility of the customer and are not
included in the order total.

Once an order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Tracking
updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the label is created.

If a package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, wait 48
hours (packages are sometimes scanned as delivered before arrival), then
contact support with your order number so we can open a carrier
investigation.
