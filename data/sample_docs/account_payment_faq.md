# Account & Payment FAQ

**Payment methods:** We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express,
PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept checks or bank transfers.

**Changing or cancelling an order:** Orders can be changed or cancelled
within 1 hour of placing them by contacting support. After 1 hour, the
order may have already entered fulfillment and cannot be modified.

**Promo codes:** Only one promo code can be applied per order. Promo
codes cannot be combined with other discounts or applied retroactively to
past orders.

**Failed payments:** If a payment fails, the order is not created and no
charge is made. Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or
the billing address not matching the card issuer's records.

**Account deletion:** Customers can request account deletion by emailing
privacy@example.com. This permanently deletes personal data within 30
days, in line with applicable data protection law. Order history required
for tax/accounting purposes may be retained in anonymized form.

**Price adjustments:** We do not offer price adjustments for items that
go on sale after purchase.
